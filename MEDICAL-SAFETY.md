# MEDICAL-SAFETY.md — Medical Safety Gate

This platform publishes health information. Every piece of content passes
through this gate before publication.

## 1. Safety scan topics

All content is scanned for:
- Medical claims
- Drug claims
- Disease claims
- Pregnancy
- Mental health
- Cardiovascular conditions
- Respiratory conditions (COPD, asthma…)
- Cancer
- Medication interactions
- Severe symptoms

## 2. Gate rules

### Rule A — Framing requirements (always)
- Medical/health conclusions carry an evidence-strength label
  (Strong/Moderate/Limited/Mixed/Insufficient).
- Community observations are labeled "Community Report Only" and never merged
  into medical claims.
- Every medical topic page states clearly that it is information, not medical advice.

### Rule B — Medication pages (NRT, varenicline, bupropion, cytisine)
Required sections:
- What it is
- How it works
- Evidence
- Effectiveness
- Common considerations
- Contraindications/warnings **where authoritative sources provide them**
- Questions to ask a healthcare professional
- Source

Absolutely forbidden:
- Prescribing to an individual
- Judging whether a user is suited to a drug
- Suggesting doses
- Replacing a doctor's advice

Every medication page ends with: "This page is information only. It does not
recommend this medication for any individual, does not suggest doses, and does
not replace professional medical advice."

### Rule C — High-risk topics
Content involving pregnancy, mental health conditions, cardiovascular or
respiratory disease, cancer, or medication interactions **does not auto-publish**.
It enters the Medical Review Queue and is published only after a careful
human-editor pass against authoritative sources, with appropriate framing and
disclaimers.

### Rule D — Severe symptoms
Pages must include a "When to seek medical advice" section where relevant and
a general escalation path: consult a healthcare professional; in an emergency
call local emergency services; for self-harm thoughts, contact a crisis line.

## 3. Medical claims vocabulary

- "Evidence shows…" → only with Strong/Moderate evidence + citation.
- "Some studies suggest…" → for Limited/Mixed evidence.
- "A community member reported…" → for experience content, never as a medical claim.
- "May cause / may help" → uncertainty language, never certainty for individual outcomes.

Forbidden phrasing anywhere:
- "This cures…", "guaranteed to work", "safe for everyone",
  "no side effects", "recommended dose: …"

## 4. Author & review honesty

We do not fabricate doctors, medical reviewers, authors or experts. If real
qualified reviewers are added in the future, their credentials will be published
truthfully. Until then, the site makes no medical-authority claims.

## 5. Disclaimer coverage

Every page footer/sidebar links to the Medical Disclaimer page, and
medication/medical pages carry an inline disclaimer block. The disclaimer page
is linked from the footer on every page.
